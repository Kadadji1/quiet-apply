## Long description (Store "Detailed description" field)

**Quiet Apply — Apply smarter, not louder.**

Quiet Apply is a free beta Chrome extension that helps you quickly understand
how well your resume fits a LinkedIn job — before you spend time applying.

When you open a LinkedIn job page, Quiet Apply automatically syncs the visible
job description. Upload or paste your resume once, and get an instant,
AI-powered breakdown:

**What you get**
- ✅ **Match score** — a quick read on how well your resume fits the role.
- 🎯 **Matched skills** — the skills you already have that the job is asking for.
- ⚠️ **Missing skills** — gaps to address or highlight before applying.
- 💡 **AI recommendations** — concrete suggestions to strengthen your fit.
- 📝 **Professional summary** — a tailored summary you can adapt for the role.

**How it works**
1. Open a LinkedIn job posting with a visible job description.
2. Quiet Apply auto-syncs the description. (You can also press **Refresh Job**.)
3. Upload or paste your resume in the popup.
4. Click analyze and review your score, skills, and recommendations.

**Good to know (honest beta notes)**
- This is a **free beta / testing version** — expect ongoing improvements.
- Works best on **LinkedIn job pages with a visible job description**.
- Job **title and company** are best-effort and may show fallback metadata; this
  does not affect the analysis, which uses the description text.
- To generate analysis, your **resume text and the job description are sent to
  the Quiet Apply backend**, which may use third-party AI providers to produce
  results.
- We do **not** collect LinkedIn passwords or login credentials.
- We do **not** sell your data.

**Privacy**
The extension only runs on LinkedIn job pages. Your resume and analysis are
stored locally in your browser, and text is sent for analysis only when you
choose to run it. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Try Quiet Apply and apply smarter, not louder.
